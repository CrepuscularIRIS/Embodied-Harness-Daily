# K703 — Curate a trainable task band and harden the sandbox against reward leakage
_parent: **Train the Policy Through, or Aligned with, the Deployment Harness** ([`harness_native_rl`](../main/harness_native_rl.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K703-trainable-task-pool-sandbox-hygiene.md` (frozen domain `embodied-agent-harness/v1`)_

## Operation
withhold verifier-only state until grading, block egress and git history, mask infrastructure failures, and keep tasks of intermediate base-policy success

- Information access: Screening rollouts give per-task difficulty; agent sees only the initialized environment.
- Transfer: Embodied analogue: hide privileged simulator state from the policy and pick tasks with nonzero student success.
- Status: candidate_pattern (2 full-text paper(s))

## When it works
- Executable binary verifier; group-relative advantage where all-0/all-1 groups give no gradient (2608.17393#s2, 2608.17528#s4)

## When it fails
- Zero-variation groups grow as the policy improves; defenses cover only observed failures; binary reward cannot credit recovery (2608.17393#s2)
- Not ablated separately from the training objective (2608.17528#s4)

## Counterexamples
- none found in the readings
