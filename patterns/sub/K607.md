# K607 — Train one model jointly under privileged and deployment-context views, deploying only the plain view
_parent: **Distil On-Policy with Logit, Privileged-Self, Agreement or Harness-Contrast Signals** ([`on_policy_distillation_objectives`](../main/on_policy_distillation_objectives.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K607-shared-param-privileged-view-joint-training.md` (frozen domain `embodied-agent-harness/v1`)_

## Operation
train one model at once on privileged-context and deployment-context prompts and deploy only the plain view

- Information access: Teacher and student share parameters; privileged context from frontier traces (2602.04942#s1) or a harness runtime control document (2610.20261#s2); Base frontier model frozen and black-box at reasoning level (2602.04942#s1).
- Transfer: Makes the student absorb harness-state information without a separate teacher model.
- Status: candidate_pattern (2 full-text paper(s))

## When it works
- Base model sometimes succeeds with the privilege; low initial KL; non-zero beta when the teacher is trained (2602.04942#s1)
- Joint interleaving, not staged (2610.20261#s2)

## When it fails
- Teacher-only training collapses when KL nears zero; student-only fails with negative privilege utility; no single best alpha (2602.04942#s1)
- T+I or T+R alone leave success unchanged; staged schedules degrade; per-harness rebuild needed (2610.20261#s2)

## Counterexamples
- none found in the readings
