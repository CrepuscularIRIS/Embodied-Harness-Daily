# R07 — Harness turns geometry into a short candidate list or written advice; the model only chooses
_parent: **Design the Frozen Model's Observation and Action Interface** ([`observe_act_interface`](../main/observe_act_interface.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source: board-induced 2026-10-21 (wave-1 weak fits)_

## Operation
keep depth, occupancy, semantic and uncertainty maps inside the harness, reduce them each step to a few navigable candidates (predicted waypoints, frontiers) or to written exploitation/exploration advice inserted into the prompt, let a frozen MLLM pick a candidate or action from images plus that list or advice (optionally with an explicit move-back option), and have a classical planner or controller reach the chosen target

- Information access: Frozen MLLMs; the model never sees the numeric maps. Candidates come from a trained waypoint predictor with a depth-derived occupancy loss (2503.10069#board) or from a depth-built BEV map limited to at most 4 frontiers, blended 0.65/0.35 with a UCB value map (2603.18210#board); advice is derived from an MLLM-scored 3D semantic map and a visibility-decayed uncertainty map, gated by a threshold (2505.08765#board).
- Contrasts: Contrasts with K102 (the model pulls numeric receipts from geometry tools on request; here the harness pushes a pre-selected list or advice) and K103 (the model proposes a free pixel target that the harness checks; here it chooses among harness-made candidates). Contrasts with K104 (a fixed action vocabulary; here the candidate set changes every step).
- Transfer: Modular navigation (frontier exploration, waypoint prediction, map-based planners) with the MLLM as a semantic chooser; transfers to navigation and search where geometry is reliable; manipulation is untested.
- Status: candidate_pattern (3 member papers, all with full text)

## When it works
- The candidate or advice channel is ablated with the model fixed: without exploitation advice total SR falls 53.50 -> 10.41 (strongest baseline 27.35); with the enhanced waypoint predictor the zero-shot navigator reaches R2R-CE SR 29 / SPL 22.46 / OSR 51 vs 19 / 16.10 / 23 for a prior zero-shot GPT-4 navigator (2505.08765#board, 2503.10069#board)
- Advice is gated rather than given every step: the gate threshold trades off SR from 38.37 (advice every step) and 49.19 (none) to 53.50 at the chosen setting (2505.08765#board)
- An explicit undo option is offered and tested on a real robot: SR 36 with backtracking vs 24 without and 20 for a supervised baseline (2503.10069#board)
- Candidate quality is measured on its own: 87.26% of predicted waypoints fall in open space vs 79.86% for the previous predictor (2503.10069#board)

## When it fails
- Settings are tuned on the evaluation tasks: the advice threshold is swept on the same tasks it is reported on, which are 605 of 2,420 tasks (25%) in a single run without variance (2505.08765#board)
- Multi-agent results are compared with single-agent baselines: a subtask counts as solved if either of two agents reaches the goal, ablations use 50 val_seen episodes rather than val_unseen, and the single-agent SR is reported as both 38.6 and 32.0 (2603.18210#board)
- Evidence is one 100-episode run, far below supervised methods (52-60 SR), with a single-step undo and a collision figure in the text that does not match the table (2503.10069#board)

## Counterexamples
- The model's choice adds little over the harness heuristic: removing VLM frontier reasoning costs only 0.562 -> 0.540 SR, while removing the second agent costs 0.562 -> 0.386 (2603.18210#board)
- More advice hurts: advice at every step (SR 38.37) is worse than no exploration advice (49.19); exploration advice leaves hard-task SPL unchanged (16.65 vs 16.68) (2505.08765#board)
- The full system's navigation error is worse than the predictor-only variant (7.01 vs 6.73) and than the prior zero-shot navigator (6.70) (2503.10069#board)
