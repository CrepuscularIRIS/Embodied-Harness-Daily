# K601 — Repair the student's failed episode from its earliest harmful step and admit only validated corrections
_parent: **Correct, Review or Relabel at Learner-Induced States** ([`learner_state_teacher_correction`](../main/learner_state_teacher_correction.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K601-first-error-teacher-repair.md` (frozen domain `embodied-agent-harness/v1`)_

## Operation
on a failed student rollout, have a black-box teacher locate the earliest harmful step, keep the prefix, replace from there, and admit the correction by an admission rule before SFT

- Information access: Teacher frozen, black-box, no logits; sees the student's trajectory and observations, no privileged channel stated (2509.14257#s1, 2610.19834#s1); Correction granularity: one step then student resumes (2509.14257#s1); every suffix step rewritten in the student's style (2610.19834#s1); bounded code macro under a repair contract (2609.32416#s1); Admission: final answer correct (2509.14257#s1); grounding filter on unobserved state (2610.19834#s1); paired-advantage lower confidence bound from snapshot restore with shared randomness (2609.32416#s1, s3); Student pilots the same harness and is trained; deployment has no teacher or verifier.
- Contrasts: Contrasts with K602 (intercept before execution, every step) and K609 (agreement reward at every state, no failure localization).
- Transfer: Needs replay or snapshot reset; embodied resets are costly and the teacher must be competent where the student fails.
- Status: candidate_pattern (3 full-text paper(s))

## When it works
- A verifiable outcome to validate corrections; student already runs the loop after BC (2509.14257#s1, 2610.19834#s1)
- Deterministic replay to re-render student-seen observations; teacher competent at student states (~70% success) (2610.19834#s1)
- Full snapshot save/restore at code decision points for verified admission (2609.32416#s1)

## When it fails
- Teacher quality limits data yield; 5K -> 10K SFT data no gain (2509.14257#s1)
- Weaker editor teachers give weaker students; student about half teacher success; live web below 8% (2610.19834#s1)
- Verification cost grows linearly with audited events (>=4,192 suffix rollouts for 19 admissions) (2609.32416#s1)
- Large trainer-seed variance; a thin admission regressed 76% -> 58%; pooling tasks causes interference (2609.32416#s2)
- Selection-rule ablation on one task with small numbers (2609.32416#s3)

## Counterexamples
- Llama3.1-8B with first-error repair SFT trails BC+GRPO and ARPO (2509.14257#s1)
- Credit weighting does not beat uniform weighting on the verified corpus (2609.32416#s2)
