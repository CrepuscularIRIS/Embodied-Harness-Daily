# R06 — Sample several candidate actions and execute the one a verifier scores highest
_parent: **Sample Candidate Actions and Let a Verifier Choose** ([`sample_verify_select`](../main/sample_verify_select.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source: board-induced 2026-10-21 (wave-1 weak fits)_

## Operation
at each decision step sample N candidate actions from the policy (VLA action samples perturbed around a fitted Gaussian, chain-of-thought action candidates, or plans with internal reflections), score each with a verifier — a learned VLM reward model, repeated true/false and masked-entity checks by the same model, or a reflector/critic — and execute the highest-scoring candidate; optionally update the policy and critic at test time from hindsight reflection on the outcome

- Information access: The policy stays frozen in one member (a VLA with a LoRA-trained 7B VLM verifier trained on 20M synthetic pairs ranked by distance to demonstration actions 2506.17811#board); the verifier is the same bias-tuned 7B model as the policy in another (2601.18492#board); a third trains the policy and critic at test time from hindsight reflections, with GPT-generated task specifications and SFT data (2602.21198#board).
- Contrasts: Contrasts with K201 (a cheap checker accepts or rejects one proposal; here several proposals are ranked and the best is executed) and K603 (a gate decides when to query a teacher; here no teacher is queried). Contrasts with K103 (the harness checks feasibility of one model-chosen target and asks to reselect).
- Transfer: Test-time scaling by sampling and verification (best-of-N, self-consistency, verifier reranking) moved to embodied actions; transfers when sampling is cheap relative to an action and the verifier ranks closer to task success than the policy's own likelihood.
- Status: candidate_pattern (3 member papers, all with full text)

## When it works
- The verifier is compared with the unverified policy on closed-loop tasks: 47.5% on a simulated benchmark (+9 points over the base VLA), 60% vs 35% (base) and 30% (another verifier) on real out-of-distribution tasks, and 56.5 vs 49.8 on a long-horizon suite; more verifier training data raises success 37.5 -> 46.3 (2506.17811#board)
- Sampling and verification are separated in the ablation: on a VLN subset no sampling gives SR 39, sampling with a vote 40 and the full dual verification 52; R2R val-unseen SR 52 / SPL 45 vs 46 / 40 for a prior LLM navigator, and REVERIE SR 29.8 vs 19.2 (2601.18492#board)
- Both reflection channels are needed: average success 33.65% vs 11.20% for a self-refinement baseline and 10.40% with both reflections removed (2602.21198#board)
- The candidate count saturates early: gains flatten beyond K=4 samples (2601.18492#board)

## When it fails
- The verifier is trained or scored on a proxy: the reward model ranks by distance to the demonstration action, a proxy for success, and the scaling argument assumes an oracle verifier against ground-truth error (2506.17811#board)
- Latency grows with the candidate count and is partly unreported: 16 candidates take 650 ms and the real system runs at about 1.5 Hz; the K x P verification overhead is not timed (2506.17811#board, 2601.18492#board)
- Ablations and real tests are small: a 90-pair subset from 8 scans without variance; 10 real trials per task (2601.18492#board, 2506.17811#board)
- Supervision comes from a frontier model with privileged information: task specifications are generated by a frontier model and the SFT data are privileged, on the authors' own benchmarks with low absolute success (2602.21198#board)
- Gains are far below trained specialists and small on another split: SR 52 vs 81 for a fully supervised navigator; +0.41 SR over a prior LLM navigator on RxR (2601.18492#board)

## Counterexamples
- A verifier can hurt: pairing the base VLA with a different published verifier scores below the base VLA alone; some tasks show no gain at all (58 vs 58, 26 vs 26) (2506.17811#board)
- Each half of the reflection scheme alone can score below using neither (2602.21198#board)
- One verification prompt adds little over voting (SR 40 -> 42 on the subset) (2601.18492#board)
