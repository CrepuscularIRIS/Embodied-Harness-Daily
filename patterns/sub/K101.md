# K101 — Let a frozen frontier MLLM steer primitive actions through a minimal observe/step tool pair
_parent: **Design the Frozen Model's Observation and Action Interface** ([`observe_act_interface`](../main/observe_act_interface.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K101-minimal-observe-step-interface.md` (frozen domain `embodied-agent-harness/v1`)_

## Operation
expose the environment as a non-advancing observe tool plus a bounded-batch step tool and leave looking, commitment size, recovery and stopping to the model

- Information access: Frozen frontier MLLM; no training, no student; Model sees only the briefing, RGB frames it requests, and step returns (executed count, budget); no pose, depth, map, waypoints or collision signal; step reports requested not realized motion; Fresh session per episode; no cross-episode memory.
- Contrasts: Contrasts with K104/K105 (same frozen model, but a semantic or code action space instead of primitives) and with K102 (same primitives but with perception receipts).
- Transfer: Needs a simulator that can return frames without advancing time and a frontier model strong enough to self-direct; no member tests moving targets, 3D action spaces or aerial embodiments.
- Status: candidate_pattern (2 full-text paper(s))

## When it works
- A strong frontier MLLM is the policy; weaker models are not the regime studied (2607.26148#s1)
- Simulated Habitat VLN-CE / ObjectNav / EQA with turn and wall-clock caps and an observe call that does not advance the simulator (2607.26148#s1, 2609.29861#s1)
- Reasoning effort (medium vs ultra) is the only lever varied with the interface fixed (2609.29861#s1)

## When it fails
- Residual failures are wrong target/route and wrong stop; target-visible ObjectNav failures (2607.26148#s1)
- Stuck forward batches with pixel-identical views and inconsistent recovery; episodes entering the goal radius but stopping elsewhere; 8/100 tasks fail in all six runs (2609.29861#s1)
- On a physical Go2: no body awareness, under-rotation without realized-motion feedback, weak persistent spatial state (2607.26148#s1)
- High inference cost and 3-19 s latency per call; benchmark contamination of proprietary models not verifiable (2607.26148#s1, 2609.29861#s1)
- 100-episode subsets, mostly single runs; no harness ablation in the reasoning-effort VLN-CE study (2607.26148#s1, 2609.29861#s1)
