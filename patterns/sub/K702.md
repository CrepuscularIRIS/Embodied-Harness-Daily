# K702 — Assemble captured harness calls into training rows by prefix rules and keep credit at rollout level
_parent: **Train the Policy Through, or Aligned with, the Deployment Harness** ([`harness_native_rl`](../main/harness_native_rl.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K702-captured-call-sequence-assembly.md` (frozen domain `embodied-agent-harness/v1`)_

## Operation
partition captured calls into append-only prefix chains or exact-prefix merges, mask harness-inserted tokens, and compute group baselines and loss normalization over rollouts rather than samples

- Information access: Only token IDs, logprobs and grouping keys; harness semantics latent; white-box policy.
- Transfer: Image pruning in embodied harnesses breaks prefix relations, which would split chains.
- Status: candidate_pattern (2 full-text paper(s))

## When it works
- Parts of the session keep append-only history (2605.24220#s2)
- Exact token-level prefix relation between consecutive calls (2608.17528#s1)
- Binary outcome reward inherited by all samples of a rollout (2608.17528#s2)

## When it fails
- Only 3 training steps profiled; cross-chain credit out of scope (2605.24220#s2)
- Template non-compositionality, retokenize drift and tool-call reserialization break prefixes (2608.17528#s1)
- Within-rollout credit left open; token-mean unstable with long negative samples; single seed (2608.17528#s2)
