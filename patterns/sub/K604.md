# K604 — Relabel learner-visited states with a privileged expert, when dynamics allow
_parent: **Correct, Review or Relabel at Learner-Induced States** ([`learner_state_teacher_correction`](../main/learner_state_teacher_correction.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K604-privileged-expert-relabel-at-learner-states.md` (frozen domain `embodied-agent-harness/v1`)_

## Operation
relabel learner-visited states with a privileged expert or geometric label generator and aggregate DAgger-style, choosing this over RL only when latent dynamics are near-deterministic

- Information access: Expert sees full latent state (2510.03207#s1, s2) or learner pose + reference route + goal region (2610.21450#s1; not a model); Student sees frame-stacked images or onboard RGB, instruction, ego state; aerial VLN in 2610.21450#s1; Optional: train the expert under extra motor noise to smooth it (2510.03207#s2).
- Contrasts: Contrasts with K603 (selective queries) and K606 (privileged self-teacher).
- Transfer: Object search and other tasks that need information gathering fall in the regime 2510.03207#s1 explicitly excludes; distillation's advantage is unproven there.
- Status: candidate_pattern (2 full-text paper(s))

## When it works
- Near-decodable POMDP without information-gathering actions; resettable simulator with latent state (2510.03207#s1)
- Expert smoothing helps only with stochastic dynamics (2510.03207#s2)
- Reference route and pose known at training time; routes followed in order (2610.21450#s1)

## When it fails
- Theory tabular with synthetic noise; active information gathering unexplored; belief contraction does not predict RL suboptimality (dog-walk) (2510.03207#s1)
- Smoothing is proof-of-concept on two tasks and vanishes under deterministic dynamics (2510.03207#s2)
- Early termination remains dominant (21-37%); reconnect-chunk collisions unanalysed; single seed (2610.21450#s1)

## Counterexamples
- none found in the readings
