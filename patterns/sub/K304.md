# K304 — Compile a successful harness trajectory into a planner-free replay card
_parent: **Build Context, Notes and Skills with the Weights Frozen** ([`harness_memory_skill_reuse`](../main/harness_memory_skill_reuse.md)) · status: case · source card: `K304-compile-trajectory-replay-card.md` (frozen domain `embodied-agent-harness/v1`)_

## Operation
compile each successful trajectory into a card with anchor-relative waypoints and VLA prompts, re-localize anchors at deployment, and replay without online planning

- Information access: No online LLM planning at deployment; a VLM localizes anchors and depth back-projects them.
- Transfer: Cheapest way to remove the teacher at deployment, but only for fixed-structure tasks.
- Status: case (1 full-text paper(s))

## When it works
- Fixed subgoal order, directly localizable objects, failures recoverable by predefined checks (2607.08448#s3)

## When it fails
- Best card selected on the reporting seeds (optimistic); new subgoals or recovery need online planning; tasks without a successful source trace score 0 (2607.08448#s3)

## Counterexamples
- none found in the readings
