# K706 — Train the policy under the same context-construction rules it will be deployed with
_parent: **Train the Policy Through, or Aligned with, the Deployment Harness** ([`harness_native_rl`](../main/harness_native_rl.md)) · status: candidate_pattern · source card: `K706-train-deploy-context-alignment.md` (frozen domain `embodied-agent-harness/v1`)_

## Operation
train the policy under the context-construction rules (compression, summaries, prompt format) used at deployment rather than applying them only at inference

- Information access: Fixed drop-on-success screenshot rule replayed in training (2608.03327#s3); summary-conditioned RL with scaffold modules attached only at evaluation (2606.14674#s2); same prompt/history/schema at train and inference (2610.15572#s3).
- Transfer: Directly addresses train/deploy scaffold mismatch, but the evidence is weak.
- Status: candidate_pattern (3 full-text paper(s))

## When it works
- Tool results already present as text make post-tool frames redundant (2608.03327#s3)
- Same demonstrations, each format used at both train and inference (2610.15572#s3)

## When it fails
- Drop rule discards the only visual evidence when a call fails semantically (2608.03327#s3)
- The summary-conditioned RL policy does not train with the exact deployment scaffold; differences may be within noise (2606.14674#s2)
- Cumulative ablation, single evaluation (2610.15572#s3)

## Counterexamples
- Held-out accuracy under compression unchanged; pre-registered +15 pp DiD not met (2608.03327#s3)
