# K610 — Prefer the teacher's harness-conditioned action over its harness-stripped action at student states
_parent: **Distil On-Policy with Logit, Privileged-Self, Agreement or Harness-Contrast Signals** ([`on_policy_distillation_objectives`](../main/on_policy_distillation_objectives.md)) · status: case · source card: `K610-harness-contrast-preference-distillation.md` (frozen domain `embodied-agent-harness/v1`)_

## Operation
at student-visited states, sample teacher actions with and without harness text, apply a pairwise preference for the harness-conditioned action, filter invalid positives by harness rules, and add response distillation

- Information access: Frozen white-box local teacher (log-probs not needed for this term); student keeps the same harness at train and deploy; No task rewards or success labels.
- Transfer: Needs a teacher cheap enough to query twice per student state.
- Status: case (1 full-text paper(s))

## When it works
- Harness text deterministic and additive; cheap local teacher; near-fully observed text environments (2610.02858#s1)
- Harness records expose checkable facts (2610.02858#s2)

## When it fails
- Gains small outside ALFWorld; untested on multimodal/embodied (2610.02858#s1)
- Cannot remove valid-but-incorrect positives (2610.02858#s2)

## Counterexamples
- none found in the readings
